# DealDuniya

Public product-discovery and review site. Visitors arrive from Instagram, Facebook, and other social posts, read an honest take, then optionally click through to a retailer.

## Product truth

- We do not sell or ship products. Purchases happen on Amazon, Flipkart, and other stores.
- Affiliate commissions are a consequence of useful pages, not the page’s only purpose.
- Do not invent ratings, tests, authors, stock, or prices.
- Demo catalogue copy is sample material until replaced with real products.

## Brand

Public name: **DealDuniya**. Existing logo and favicon in `public/brand` and `public/` are the source of truth. Do not generate replacements.

## What must stay working

Admin, Prisma/Postgres, `/go/` click tracking, contact and newsletter forms, blog, legal pages, auth cookies, sitemap, robots.
